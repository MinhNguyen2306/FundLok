FundLok × VPBank — escrow lifecycle, onboarding to loan closure

Nine steps, five lanes, and every point where FundLok's systems must talk to VPBank's.

Draft v0.1 · 4 August 2026 · one escrow account per loan project, held FBO the borrower and its lenders

The account structure. Each funded project gets its own VPBank escrow account titled [FL_LEGAL_ENTITY] FBO [PROJECT_CODE]. FundLok is the account holder of record — it opens, operates and instructs the account, and is the entity VPBank contracts with and performs CDD on. The beneficiaries are one SME borrower and that project's N lenders, who hold the economic interest. They are counterparties to each other under the loan contract, not VPBank account holders. FundLok never holds customer funds, and no project's money can ever settle another project's obligation.

The nine capabilities FundLok needs from VPBank

  • B1Escrow account openingShould have
  • B2Account name verificationMust have
  • B3Inbound credit notificationMust have
  • B4Payment initiation, single & batchMust have
  • B5Balance inquiryMust have
  • B6Statement retrievalMust have
  • B7Payment status queryShould have
  • B8Escrow account closureNice to have
  • B9Consent & OAuth 2.0 authorisationMust have

Every capability has a defined manual fallback so the flow can run before the API is complete. The highest-leverage ask is not on this list: per-lender virtual account numbers under a project escrow account, which would make inbound attribution deterministic and remove reference-matching risk entirely.
