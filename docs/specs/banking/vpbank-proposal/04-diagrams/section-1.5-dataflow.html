Section 1.5 — Data flow

What crosses the boundary between FundLok and VPBank, in which direction, and what deliberately does not.

FundLok moves data and instructions, not money. Funds move only between the parties' own accounts and the escrow account held at VPBank.

Parties FundLok VPBank data or instruction direct to the bank, bypassing FundLok
