Section 1.4 — the closed loop, and the gate on FundLok's fee

Money never leaves the escrow perimeter except to an account already registered as a permitted destination. FundLok's fee is a line inside a beneficiary payment, not a payment of its own.

Working case: loan of VND 2.5 billion, eight participating investors, 1% disbursement fee. FundLok internal estimates dated 5 August 2026.

The four gates

#GateCheck appliedConsequence
1DestinationIs every beneficiary on the registered permitted-destination list?Fails on any account not on the list — including FundLok's own.
2PairingDoes the fee line sit inside an instruction that also pays a beneficiary?Fails on a fee line standing alone, or any draw on an idle balance.
3Proportion and ceilingIs the fee line within the registered proportion of this instruction, and within the registered ceiling?Arithmetic, not a representation. Both values to be confirmed with VPBank.
4HoldIs either account held under the dispute mechanism?If held, the fee line is suspended for the duration — the beneficiary line still settles.
