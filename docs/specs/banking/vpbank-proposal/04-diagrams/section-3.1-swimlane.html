Section 3.1 — Nine-step end-to-end process

From party onboarding to loan closure, with the accountable owner named for every step.

Four parties, four distinct roles, no party holding two roles. Money moves only through the custodian; FundLok moves instructions and data, never funds.

Investor — lender of record SME — borrower FundLok — arranger & servicer VPBank — custodian
