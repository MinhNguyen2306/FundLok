## Quy tắc trình bày

1. **Văn phong trang trọng.** Formal register throughout. No colloquialisms, no contractions.
2. **Giữ nguyên cấu trúc Markdown.** Same headings with the same numbers, same tables with the
   same number of columns and the same number of rows, same bold and italic emphasis, same
   bullet and numbered lists. Do not merge or split tables.
3. **Giữ nguyên toàn bộ chữ số đúng như bản gốc** — including thousands separators and decimal
   points exactly as written in English. Do not convert 2,500,000,000 to 2.500.000.000. A
   Vietnamese reviewer will adjust number formatting in a later pass.
4. **Không thêm, không bớt, không tóm tắt.** Translate everything. Add nothing.
5. Dates stay in the source form (e.g. 2026-08-12, 5 August 2026).
6. Cross-references to sections keep their numbers unchanged (Section 1.4 → Mục 1.4).
7. On first use of an unavoidable technical English term not in the list above, give the
   Vietnamese followed by the English in parentheses.
